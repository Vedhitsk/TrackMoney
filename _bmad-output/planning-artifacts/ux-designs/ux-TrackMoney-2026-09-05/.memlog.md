- (override) CORRECTION: I earlier claimed the shipped dark theme's surface sits '1.36:1' above its background. That number was asserted, not computed, and it is wrong — the real ratio is 1.10:1. More importantly the METRIC was wrong: at near-black the +0.05 flare term in the WCAG formula dominates, so any two dark greys score ~1.1:1 regardless of how different they look. Surface separation must be measured in perceptual L*. Real figures for the shipped dark theme: bg->surface dL* 4.83, surface->elevated dL* 3.56, against a 6-9 target. Still genuinely shallow, but for a reason contrast ratio cannot express. color-themes-1.html corrected and marked SUPERSEDED.
