TrackMoney — primary material & colour directions

Four directions, each a complete system in light and dark. The primary accent is no longer a flat fill — it is a material, built the way the button you attached is built.

The material, in four layers: a vertical gradient ramp (light stop → brand → deep stop); a 1px rim-light along the top inner edge, so the control reads as lit from above; a coloured bloom beneath it rather than a neutral grey shadow; and a translucent rounded tile carrying the glyph. Pressed state compresses the ramp, tightens the bloom and drops 2px.
Brand and category are separate systems. The brand material is chrome only — FAB, primary CTA, active tab, active segment. Categories are drawn from a 12-hue master palette, and each direction automatically keeps the 10 furthest from its own brand hue in CIE Lab. Two floors are enforced: ΔE ≥ 28 from brand, and ≥ 3:1 fill contrast on the card surface.

Where the shipped build stands

SUPERSEDED — this board explored four flat-fill hue directions. It was replaced by primary-material-3.html, which follows the gradient-pill reference instead. Kept for the audit of the shipped palette below, which still stands.

Three measured problems. Tertiary text (placeholders like “Add a note”) fails contrast in both modes. Brand violet is also category colour #1 — ΔE 0 — so the FAB and your largest spending slice are the same colour. And the primary controls have no material at all: flat fill, neutral shadow.

Correction. An earlier version of this page said dark cards sat “only 1.36:1” above the background. That figure was wrong, and the metric was the wrong one to use: at near-black the +0.05 flare term in the WCAG formula dominates, so any two dark greys score about 1.1:1 no matter how different they look. Surface separation is properly measured in perceptual L*. On that measure the shipped dark theme steps background → surface by ΔL* 4.83 and surface → elevated by ΔL* 3.56, against a 6–9 target — genuinely shallow, but for a reason the ratio column below cannot show.
